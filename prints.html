How it works

  1. Pick a frame here or in the galleries. For one from the galleries, open the photo and copy its file name, for example andes-glaciers-02.jpg.
  2. Tell me what you have in mind: where it will hang and any size or finish you already know you want.
  3. I reply with a quote. Size, material, finish and delivery are settled in that conversation.

Prints FAQ

Is there a price list?

No. Every print is quoted individually: you tell me the frame and what you have in mind, and I reply with a quote.

Can I ask for a photo that is not on this page?

Possibly. Send me the file name from the gallery or describe the photo. I check the frame and its rights before I quote. Frames with identifiable people, and events shot under media accreditation, are only offered as prints when the releases and event terms allow it.

What sizes, papers and finishes are available?

Tell me where the print will hang and what you have in mind. Size, material, finish and delivery are settled in the quote conversation.

Is a print the same as a licence?

No. A print is a copy for display. Publishing or reproducing an image, in print or online, needs a licence.
