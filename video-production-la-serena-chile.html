How it works

  1. Brief. You say what is being delivered or opened, the dates, and how you will use the material.
  2. Written quote. You get the scope, filming days, deliverables and deadlines before any date is reserved.
  3. Filming. Shooting follows the real progress of the works and the event programme.
  4. Delivery. Press photos first, then the short cut, then the full film, as agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Does TapePhoto make videos or only photographs?

Both. Besides photography, Carlos films projects and covers company events in La Serena, Coquimbo and the rest of the Coquimbo Region.

Do you work with companies based outside Chile?

Yes. Coordination is by email or video call, in English or Spanish, and the scope is set in a written quote before any date is reserved.

Can you deliver photos on the day of the event?

Yes, a selection of press photos can be delivered the same day. Deadlines for the remaining photos and the videos are set in the quote.

Does the drone need permits?

Flights are planned under the rules of the DGAC, Chile's civil aviation authority. If a location has restrictions, that is checked before aerial shots are promised.

Can we get the footage without narration to make our own versions?

Yes. Clean versions and selected interview clips can be delivered so your team can translate or re-edit. This is agreed in the quote.

Tell me what you need filmed.

Once I know what is being delivered or opened, where and when, I can advise you and prepare the quote.

  • Written quote before any date is reserved
  • Photo and video from one local supplier
  • Coordination in English or Spanish
  • Usage licence agreed per project, in writing
