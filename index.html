Notes

What photography services do you offer in La Serena and Coquimbo?

Four services: aerial and drone photography, a real estate photo plus drone package (interiors, exteriors and aerials in one visit), motorsport and event coverage, and business and brand sessions with team portraits, workspace and product shots. There are dedicated Spanish-language pages for real estate, for hotels, cabins and tourism, and for businesses.

How is a photo session quoted?

Every quote is custom. Tell Carlos the type of session, the location and the date, and he replies on WhatsApp with a quote for that scope.

Which areas do you cover?

Carlos is based in Coquimbo and works across La Serena, Coquimbo and the whole Coquimbo Region in Chile. Sessions elsewhere in Chile are possible, with travel quoted separately.

Are drone flights allowed at my location?

Drone flights are planned according to DGAC (Chilean civil aviation) rules and the conditions of each site. Locations near airports, over crowds or in protected areas may need extra permits and lead time, so mention the location when you get in touch.

How do I book a session?

Message Carlos on WhatsApp at +1 (442) 385-4585, use the contact form on the Contact page, or email carlos@netwebmedia.com. Tell him the type of session, the location and the date, and he replies with a quote.
